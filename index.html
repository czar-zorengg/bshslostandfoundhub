How It Works

Four simple steps to get a lost item back to its owner.

1

Report

Lost something, or found something? Submit a quick report with details and an optional photo.

2

Review

School staff review the report and approve found items so they appear publicly.

3

Browse & Claim

Students browse approved found items and submit a claim request with proof of ownership.

4

Reunite

Staff verify the claim and approve it — the item is marked returned to its owner.
